Lab-quality pathology, close to the point of care.

UnaVix brings automated slide staining and remote review by pathologists to clinics without a laboratory or an on-site pathologist. This gives remote clinics access to basic pathology exams, with little specialist expertise needed on site.

Partner with us Our technologies

One set of tools, many tests

Our technologies are compatible with:

Cytology Pap smears and liquid-based cytology, for example for cervical cancer screening.
Histology H&E-stained tissue sections, read remotely by pathologists.
Fine-needle aspiration Stained aspirates from lumps and lymph nodes, read remotely by pathologists.

Our technologies

GyroStainer Our low-cost automated stainer, validated in the lab at the Geneva University Hospitals. A field pilot is planned. Telepathology portal Remote review of digitised slides by pathologists, with results sent back to the clinic. ML for cervical samples Assists pathologists in reading cervical cytology samples.

Next steps

  1. Pilot in CameroonAs part of a study led by the Geneva University Hospitals (HUG), we will test GyroStainer and the telepathology portal on cervical cytology samples in the field. The samples collected in the pilot will also help us train our machine learning algorithm.
  2. Histology validationNext, we will validate GyroStainer and the telepathology portal on histology samples.
