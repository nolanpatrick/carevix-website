Cervical cancer screening and treatment, close to point of care.

CareVix brings cervical cancer screening, on-site triage and treatment of precancer to communities far from a laboratory, in a mobile or a fixed clinic.

Our solutions How it works

In 2022, cervical cancer took the lives of 348,189 women.

Almost 94% of those deaths were in low- and middle-income countries. It is one of the most preventable cancers, but many women there cannot reach the services that would prevent it.

Three gaps stand between these women and care:

Results take weeks

Samples travel to a central laboratory. Many women cannot make the long journey back for their results and are lost before treatment.

Too few pathologists

Reading cytology needs trained cytotechnologists and pathologists, who are scarce and concentrated in city hospitals.

Manual staining varies

Pap staining by hand depends on timing and handling. Inconsistent slides are harder to read and less reliable.

Sources: Bray F. et al., Global cancer statistics 2022 (GLOBOCAN), CA Cancer J Clin, 2024. World Health Organization, Global strategy to accelerate the elimination of cervical cancer, 2020.

One complete pathway, on site

We follow the WHO's screen, triage and treat approach. A woman can go from her first test to treatment without ever travelling to a city laboratory.

1 · Screen

HPV self-test

Each woman takes her own sample in private, and it is tested for high-risk HPV on site.

2 · Triage

Cytology, read remotely

A cervical sample is taken from HPV-positive women and prepared on a slide. Our automated Pap stainer stains it on site, and a pathologist reads it through our telecytology portal.

3 · Treat

Thermal ablation

Women with precancerous lesions are treated on site. Suspected cancers are referred to a hospital.
