GyroStainer

A compact robot that stains Pap slides the same way, every time. It brings lab-quality cytology triage to places that have no cytology lab.

Run a protocol Ask about GyroStainer

Watch a Pap stain happen

Ten stations sit in a ring. A rotating arm carries the slide holder from bath to bath, dipping, rinsing and shaking the slides at each one. Here it is, sped up.

Six slides loaded.

Press run to send them through the twelve steps of a Pap stain.

The smear takes on the stain colours as it goes.

Designed for small labs and hard conditions

Up to 6 slides per runSized for decentralised triage, where batches are small and reagents are precious.
About 21 minutes per runFast enough for same-day results.
Open reagents, standard beakersNo proprietary cartridges. Reagents can be sourced locally and baths lift out for cleaning.
Nine baths and a drying stationThe full Pap sequence, including rinses and fan drying, in one compact ring.
Touchscreen and browser controlPick a stored protocol on the device, or edit protocols from a web browser.
Works offlineNo continuous internet connection needed to run.

Tested on real slides

At the Geneva University Hospitals, GyroStainer stained 24 patient-derived cytology slides across four runs. HUG pathologists found the nuclear detail and cytoplasmic staining clear and interpretable.

Faster review, with AI in the loop

With EPFL's LTS5 lab, we are researching an algorithm that highlights suspicious cells on a digitised slide, so experts know where to look first.

It is part of our telecytology platform as a research tool. Once validated, it will be used in every deployment to save reviewers time, at no extra cost to our partners.

Available on its own

GyroStainer is part of every Wave and Loop setup, and cytology laboratories can also acquire it as a standalone device. Get in touch to talk about your laboratory's needs.
