GyroStainer

A compact auto-stainer that delivers consistent staining every time. It can run different staining protocols. In CareVix it runs the Pap stain, bringing lab-quality cytology triage to places that have no cytology lab.

Watch a Pap stain happen

Ten stations sit in a ring. A rotating arm carries the slide holder from bath to bath, dipping the slides at each one. Here is our Pap protocol, sped up.

Four slides loaded

    The smear takes on the stain colours as it goes. The real protocol takes about 21 minutes.

    Designed for small labs and hard conditions

    Up to 4 slides per runSized for decentralised triage, where batches are small and reagents are precious.
    About 21 minutes for a Pap stainFor our Pap protocol, fast enough for same-day results. Run times for other protocols depend on the protocol.
    Open reagents, standard beakersNo proprietary cartridges. Reagents can be sourced locally and baths lift out for cleaning.
    Nine baths and a drying stationEnough for a full Pap sequence, including rinses and fan drying, in one compact ring.
    Touchscreen and browser controlPick a stored protocol on the device, or create and edit protocols from a web browser.
    Works offlineNo continuous internet connection needed to run.

    Tested on real slides

    At the Geneva University Hospitals, GyroStainer stained 24 patient-derived cytology slides across four runs. HUG pathologists found the nuclear detail and cytoplasmic staining clear and interpretable.

    Faster review, with AI in the loop

    With EPFL's LTS5 lab, we are researching an algorithm that highlights suspicious cells on a digitised slide, so pathologists know where to look first.

    It is part of our telecytology platform as a research tool. Once validated, it will be used in every deployment to save reviewers time, at no extra cost to our partners.

    Available on its own

    GyroStainer is part of every Wave and Loop setup, and cytology laboratories can also acquire it as a standalone device. Get in touch to talk about your laboratory's needs.
