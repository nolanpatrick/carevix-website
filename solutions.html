Wave and Loop

Two complete screen-and-treat solutions, from the first conversation to treatment. Same quality of care, different rhythm. Pick one to see how a day works.

Wave: high volume, two days

Built for sites where many women come for screening. Samples are tested in batches, so results and treatment follow the next day.

Loop: same-day care

Built for smaller or remote communities. Each sample is tested on its own, so a woman can be screened and treated in a single visit.

A day with Wave

Day one

  1. Set upThe team prepares the unit or clinic room and the testing equipment.
  2. Welcome and counsellingWomen are registered and the process is explained in a private conversation.
  3. Sample collectionA nurse collects a sample from each woman throughout the day.
  4. HPV testing in batchesSamples are tested together on a batch HPV platform, designed for high volumes.
  5. Staining of positive samplesSlides from HPV-positive women are stained on site with GyroStainer.
  6. Remote reviewSlides are digitised and sent to cytology experts for review.

Day two

  1. ResultsWomen come back to receive their results in person.
  2. TreatmentWomen with CIN2 or CIN3 lesions are treated on site.
  3. ReferralSuspected cancers are referred to a hospital.

A day with Loop

One visit

  1. Set upThe team prepares the unit or clinic room and the testing equipment.
  2. Welcome and counsellingWomen are registered and the process is explained in a private conversation.
  3. Sample collectionA nurse collects a sample from each woman.
  4. Rapid HPV testEach sample is tested individually on a rapid cartridge-based platform.
  5. Staining of positive samplesSlides from HPV-positive women are stained on site with GyroStainer.
  6. Remote reviewSlides are digitised and reviewed by cytology experts the same day.
  7. TreatmentWomen with CIN2 or CIN3 lesions are treated before they go home.
  8. ReferralSuspected cancers are referred to a hospital.

On wheels or within walls

Both solutions run in a mobile unit that moves from village to village, or in a fixed clinic or laboratory. The workflow and equipment are the same, so a programme can start mobile and settle in one place, or the other way round.

Works best alongside awareness

CareVix doesn't run awareness campaigns, but screening reaches more women when the community knows it's coming and why it matters. We recommend pairing a deployment with a local awareness campaign, organised by our partners.

Patient data, handled with care

Remote review means health data travels. We design every step so that it stays protected.

Only what is needed

Slides are shared for review with the information required for a diagnosis and nothing more.

Limited access

Only the clinicians involved in a woman's care can see her results.

Local rules first

Data handling follows the regulations of the country where we work, agreed with each partner before deployment.
