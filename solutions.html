Solutions

The complete pathology workflow

We know that implementing a pathology workflow in a clinic or hospital is complicated. Alongside our own enabling technologies, GyroStainer and the telepathology portal, we can supply everything else it takes, from sample preparation to remote reading, and support you in putting it all in place.

From sample to diagnosis

Whether you want to automate your laboratory, read slides remotely or build an entire screening programme, we can supply any part of the workflow. Every part can be combined into a package that fits your setting.

1 · Prepare

Vortex mixer and centrifuge

To prepare liquid-based cytology samples before staining.

2 · Stain Our technology

GyroStainer

Our automated stainer: Pap, H&E or Giemsa, six slides per run. We also supply the slides and staining reagents for each protocol.

3 · Digitise

Digital microscope

Scans each stained slide into a high-resolution image.

4 · Read Our technology

Telepathology portal

Pathologists review the slides remotely and send the results back to the clinic. You can use your own pathologists or UnaVix pathologists.

For clinics without reliable power or internet

We know many clinics face power cuts or have no reliable internet, so we can supply both.

Backup power

An uninterruptible power supply (UPS) keeps staining and scanning running through power cuts.

Satellite internet

A Starlink connection links clinics without reliable internet to the portal.

Support at every step

Included in every package: dedicated assistance to put the workflow in place and keep it running.

  1. PlanWe look at your setting, the samples you handle and your power and internet supply, and design a workflow that fits.
  2. Set up and trainWe help set up the equipment and train your staff to prepare, stain and digitise slides.
  3. Ongoing assistanceWe stay available to help as your laboratory or programme runs and grows.
