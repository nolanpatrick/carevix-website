Wave and Loop

Two complete screen-triage-treat solutions. Same quality of care, different rhythm.

ScreenTriageTreat

Wave: high volume, two days

Built for sites that many women can reach, like cities or large towns. Samples are tested in batches, so triage and treatment follow the next day.

The Wave workflow

Day one: screen

  1. Set upThe team prepares the unit or clinic room and the testing equipment.
  2. Welcome and counsellingWomen are registered and the process is explained in a private conversation.
  3. HPV self-samplingEach woman collects a vaginal sample herself, in private, throughout the day.
  4. HPV testing in batchesSamples are tested together on a batch HPV platform, designed for high volumes.

Day two: triage and treat

  1. ResultsHPV-positive women come back to receive their results in person.
  2. Cervical sampleA nurse collects a cervical sample from each HPV-positive woman.
  3. Staining on siteSlides are Pap-stained on site with GyroStainer.
  4. Remote reviewSlides are digitised and read by pathologists on our telecytology platform.
  5. TreatmentWomen with CIN2 or CIN3 lesions are treated on site with thermal ablation.
  6. ReferralSuspected cancers, and lesions that can't be treated with thermal ablation, are referred to a hospital.

Loop: lower volume, same-day care

Built for smaller or remote communities. Each sample is tested individually, so a woman can be screened and treated in a single visit.

The Loop workflow

One visit: screen, triage and treat

  1. Set upThe team prepares the unit or clinic room and the testing equipment.
  2. Welcome and counsellingWomen are registered and the process is explained in a private conversation.
  3. HPV self-samplingEach woman collects a vaginal sample herself, in private.
  4. Rapid HPV testEach sample is tested individually on a rapid cartridge-based platform.
  5. Cervical sampleA nurse collects a cervical sample from each HPV-positive woman.
  6. Staining on siteSlides are Pap-stained on site with GyroStainer.
  7. Remote reviewSlides are digitised and read by pathologists the same day.
  8. TreatmentWomen with CIN2 or CIN3 lesions are treated with thermal ablation before they go home.
  9. ReferralSuspected cancers, and lesions that can't be treated with thermal ablation, are referred to a hospital.

On wheels or within walls

Wave and Loop can each run as a mobile unit that moves from village to village, or in a fixed clinic or laboratory. A mobile Wave uses two vans, and a mobile Loop uses one.
