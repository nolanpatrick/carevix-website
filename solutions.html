Wave and Loop

Two complete screen-triage-treat solutions. Same quality of care, different rhythm.

Wave: high volume, two days

Built for sites that many women can reach, like cities or large towns. Samples are tested in batches, so triage and treatment follow the next day.

Loop: lower volume, same-day care

Built for smaller or remote communities. Each sample is tested individually, so a woman can be screened and treated in a single visit.

How the days work

Screen

Day 1

  1. Set upThe team prepares the vans or clinic room and the testing equipment.
  2. RegistrationWomen are registered and receive a labelled sample kit. A nurse shows them how to take the swab.
  3. HPV self-testEach woman takes a vaginal swab herself, in private. The samples are tested together on the careHPV system, up to 90 in one run.
  4. HPV resultsHPV-negative women are informed and can go home. HPV-positive women are invited back the next day.

Same day

  1. Set upThe team prepares the van or clinic room and the testing equipment.
  2. RegistrationWomen are registered and receive a labelled sample kit. A nurse shows them how to take the swab.
  3. HPV self-testEach woman takes a vaginal swab herself, in private. Each sample is tested individually on the GeneXpert, with a result in about an hour.
  4. HPV resultsHPV-negative women are informed and can go home. HPV-positive women stay for triage.
Triage

Day 2

  1. Cytology triageHPV-positive women return in the morning. A nurse collects a cervical sample, which is Pap-stained on site with GyroStainer.
  2. Remote reviewSlides are digitised and read by a pathologist on our telecytology platform. The first results arrive late in the morning.

Same day

  1. Cytology triageA nurse collects a cervical sample from each HPV-positive woman, which is Pap-stained on site with GyroStainer.
  2. Remote reviewSlides are digitised and read by a pathologist on our telecytology platform, while the woman waits.
Treat

Day 2

  1. Thermal ablationWomen with CIN2 or CIN3 lesions are treated as soon as they receive their result.
  2. ReferralSuspected cancers, and lesions that can't be treated with thermal ablation, are referred to a hospital.

Same day

  1. Thermal ablationWomen with CIN2 or CIN3 lesions are treated before they go home.
  2. ReferralSuspected cancers, and lesions that can't be treated with thermal ablation, are referred to a hospital.

On wheels or within walls

Wave and Loop can each run as a mobile unit that moves from village to village, or in a fixed clinic or laboratory. A mobile Wave uses two vans, and a mobile Loop uses one.
