How a Wave clinic works

  1. Day 1Self-sample and HPV testWomen take their own sample in private. All samples are tested together in one batch.

Device

GyroStainer

A compact auto-stainer that applies the Pap stain the same way every time, so slides are easier to read and results more reliable. It can also run other staining protocols.

Inside every Wave and Loop clinic, and available on its own. Existing laboratories and clinics can use GyroStainer to make their staining consistent and accurate, enabling faster cytology results.

Ask about GyroStainer

Our pre-validated Pap stain protocol

Ten stations sit in a ring. A rotating arm carries the slide holder from bath to bath, dipping the slides at each one. Here is the protocol, sped up.

Four slides loaded

    The smear takes on the stain colours as it goes. The protocol takes about 25 minutes.

    Designed for small labs and hard conditions

    Up to 4 slides per runSized for decentralised triage, where batches are small and reagents are precious.
    About 25 minutes for a Pap stainFast enough for same-day results. Run times for other protocols depend on the protocol.

    Tested on real slides

    At the Geneva University Hospitals, GyroStainer stained 24 patient-derived cytology slides across four runs. HUG pathologists found the nuclear detail and cytoplasmic staining clear and interpretable.

    Software

    Telecytology portal

    Our trusted network of pathologists reviews every slide remotely and returns results quickly, so women can be treated straight away. No pathologist is needed on site.

    Inside every Wave and Loop clinic, and available on its own. Clinics and laboratories without a pathologist can send their slides through the portal and receive expert results quickly.

    From stained slide to result

    1. DigitiseA digital microscope scans the stained slide into a high-resolution image.
    2. UploadThe image is uploaded to the portal automatically, with the clinical information needed for a diagnosis.
    3. Review and returnA pathologist from our network reads the slide. The result and recommendation go straight back to the site, ready for treatment.

    A simple interface

    Patient data stays protected

    A woman's name never leaves the site. The portal only sees a random code, the slide and the clinical information needed for a diagnosis.

    Step 1 of 6

    1. 1RegisterThe nurse enters the woman's details in separate on-site software, which creates a random code. Only the code and clinical information go to the portal.
    2. 2LabelA QR code holding only the code goes on her slide. The slide carries no name.
    3. 3ScanThe scanned image syncs to the portal over an encrypted connection and is matched to her clinical information by its code.
    4. 4ReviewThe pathologist sees the slide with what's needed for a diagnosis, such as age, HPV result, HIV status and history.
    5. 5ReportThe result and report go back to the site, tagged only with the code.
    6. 6MatchThe on-site software links the code to her name, and the nurse gives her the result.
    Encrypted on site and in transit Informed consent from every woman Follows local data regulations

    In development

    Building a machine learning algorithm to speed up review

    With EPFL's LTS5 lab, we are developing an algorithm that analyses every digitised slide and points the pathologist straight to the most suspicious cells. The pathologist always makes the diagnosis.

    Research preview
    1. 1DivideThe digital slide is split into small tiles.
    2. 2AnalyseEvery cell in every tile is isolated and given an abnormality score.
    3. 3RankThe cells with the highest scores are flagged, up to 40 per slide.
    4. 4ReviewThe pathologist goes straight to the flagged cells and makes the diagnosis.
    About 1 hourto review a full slide image by hand
    2 minutesfor the algorithm to analyse a slide
    About 10 minutesto reach a diagnosis by going straight to the flagged cells

    Early research figures. Once validated, the algorithm will be built into the portal at no extra cost to our partners.
