The people who made it possible

GyroStainer exists thanks to two institutions and the people in them.

Signal Processing Laboratory 5 (LTS5), EPFL

LTS5 provided the institutional and financial support that made GyroStainer possible, and continues to back CareVix.

Geneva University Hospitals (HUG)

HUG's clinicians defined the workflow, guided the design and evaluated the slides GyroStainer produced.

Why we do this

Cervical cancer is one of the few cancers we know how to prevent. What's missing in many places isn't knowledge, it's a pathway that works from the first test to treatment.

We focus on that pathway: reliable triage on site, expert review from a distance, and treatment before a woman goes home.

Let's bring screening and treatment closer to the women who need it.
