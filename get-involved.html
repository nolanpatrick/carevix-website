For NGOs and health programmes

You bring knowledge of the communities and the programme. We bring the screen-triage-treat solution, and we plan the deployment with you.

  • A solution that fitsWave for high-demand sites, Loop for smaller or remote ones, in a mobile unit or a fixed clinic.
  • The full pathwayHPV self-testing, on-site cytology triage, remote review and thermal ablation of CIN2 and CIN3.
