Let's work on this together

Whether you run health programmes or want to fund them, there's a place for you in what we're building.

For NGOs and health programmes

You bring knowledge of the communities and the programme. We bring the screen-and-treat solution, and we plan the deployment with you.

  • A solution that fitsWave for high-demand sites, Loop for smaller or remote ones, in a mobile unit or a fixed clinic.
  • The full pathwayFrom HPV testing to on-site staining, remote expert review and treatment of CIN2 and CIN3.
  • GyroStainer on its ownFor laboratories that only need reliable, automated Pap staining.
  • Planned togetherWe start with a conversation about the setting, the expected number of women and the existing health services.

For investors and funders

Cervical cancer can be eliminated. Your support turns a working prototype and a proven workflow into deployments that reach women.

  • Pilot deploymentsFund the first Wave and Loop sites with partner NGOs.
  • Clinical validationSupport the next GyroStainer studies: comparison with standard staining, repeatability and pilots in low-resource laboratories.
  • AI-assisted reviewHelp validate the algorithm that speeds up expert review.
  • A strong foundationA team rooted in EPFL engineering and HUG clinical expertise.
